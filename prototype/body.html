    About this prototype

    Demo content only

    Every stall is a labeled sample. No real businesses, names or photos are used. Real photos, supplied by each approved business, will appear in the profile frames.

    Street and stalls are separate

    The architecture is one illustration; each stall is its own component drawn from a business record. Adding, moving or editing a business never touches the street.
