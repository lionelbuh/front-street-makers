Who can apply

Category A

Home-Based Business

Home bakeries and Cottage Food, handmade products, candles and soaps, jewelry and accessories, arts and crafts, personalized gifts, small home-based producers and home-based services.

Category B

Independent Micro-Business

Independent artisans, small workshops and studios, local makers and creators, appointment-based and pop-up businesses, independent service providers, and owner-operated businesses without a conventional retail storefront.

We list small, independent, owner-operated businesses in Temecula, Murrieta and nearby communities. Franchises, chains and conventional large retail businesses are not eligible.
