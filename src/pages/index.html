    Why we started

    Our story →

    A booth fee, the insurance, the permits, the tent and a whole Saturday: for a home baker or candle maker, a market day can cost a big part of what a good day earns.

    Front Street Makers gives local makers a stall that costs nothing, so neighbors can find them every day of the week, not only when they can afford a booth. It's run independently by a Temecula home-based business owner who knows those numbers first-hand.

    How Front Street Makers works

    For neighbors

    Discover, then buy direct

    Open a stall to meet the maker, see their work and contact them through their own website, ordering page or socials. We never take orders or payments.

    For makers

    100% free, and fair

    Listings are completely free: no sign-up fee, subscription, commission or paid ranking. Approved businesses take turns on the street, new ones get a welcome boost, and everyone stays in the directory.

    Two kinds of businesses

    Home-based and micro

    Home bakeries, Cottage Food, handmade goods and home services, plus independent artisans, studios, pop-ups and owner-operated services without a storefront.
