Listing terms

Free listings, fair rules.

  1. Free. Listings are free. There are no subscriptions or commissions, and nobody pays for a better position.
  2. Who can be listed. Home-based businesses and independent, owner-operated micro-businesses in Temecula, Murrieta and nearby communities. Franchises, chains and conventional large retail businesses are not eligible.
  3. Review. Every application is reviewed by hand. We may ask questions, suggest edits, or decline a listing that doesn't fit the marketplace.
  4. Your responsibilities. You are responsible for following the laws, licenses, permits, tax and safety rules that apply to your business. Front Street Makers is not a licensing authority and does not verify licenses. Businesses still clarifying their requirements are published once their situation is clear.
  5. Your content. You confirm that you own, or have permission to share, the text and photos you send, and you allow us to show them on your listing and in promotion of the marketplace. You can ask us to remove them at any time.
  6. No sales through us. Customers contact and buy from you directly. We never take orders, payments or commissions, and we are not part of your transactions.
  7. Visibility. Approved businesses are always in the directory. Spots on the illustrated street rotate fairly each day.
  8. Sponsors. Paid sponsor placements are separate from listings, always labeled “Sponsor”, and never change the order of the directory.
  9. Independent. Front Street Makers is an independent online marketplace. It is not affiliated with the City of Temecula, Old Town Temecula, its merchants association or its farmers market.
  10. Changes. We may update these terms; we'll let listed businesses know about important changes.
