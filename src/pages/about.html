Our story

Why Front Street Makers exists.

Because some of the best things made in the Temecula Valley come out of home kitchens and garage workshops, and the people who make them often can't afford a place to show them.

The math of a market day

A home baker, a candle maker or a jewelry maker works on thin margins. Before selling a single item at a farmers market, a fair or a pop-up, the costs add up:

  • the booth fee, sometimes plus a share of sales;
  • the liability insurance most organizers require;
  • event permits and, for food, county health permits;
  • a tent, a table, weights, signs and packaging;
  • gas, and a whole day away from making.

For many small makers, these costs eat a big part of what a good day earns, and on a slow or rainy day they can take all of it. That's a hard way to start a business, and it keeps wonderful local makers invisible.

So we built a stall that costs nothing

Front Street Makers gives every eligible home-based business and independent micro-business a stall on an illustrated version of Old Town Temecula's Front Street, for free. No sign-up fee, no subscription, no commission on sales, no paid ranking. Neighbors discover you, then contact and buy from you directly, through your own website, ordering page or social accounts.

Every approved business stays in the directory, and the stalls on the street take turns every day, so a brand-new home bakery gets the same chance to be seen as anyone else.

Inspired by European town markets

In the small towns of Switzerland and France, the weekly market is where you meet the people who make things: the cheese maker, the baker, the woman with the honey. We wanted that same feeling for the Temecula Valley, set under the arch and along the boardwalks of Old Town.

Alongside the markets we love

We're not here to replace farmers markets, fairs or craft shows. They're wonderful, and many of our makers sell there too. Front Street Makers is a place to be seen the other six days of the week, and a free first step for makers who aren't ready to pay for a booth yet.

Who runs it, and how it stays free

Front Street Makers is run independently by a Temecula home-based business owner who knows these numbers first-hand. A few local sponsors, always clearly labeled, and an optional $100 first-website service help cover the costs. Listings themselves will always be free.

We're not affiliated with the City of Temecula, Old Town Temecula or its farmers market.
