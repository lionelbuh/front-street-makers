Privacy notice

Your information, plainly.

We collect only what we need to review and publish free business listings, and we never sell it.

What we collect

On purpose, almost nothing private. We ask businesses for information they already share publicly, and only two private details:

  • Public business details: business name, what you offer, a description, your city or service area, and the contacts you choose to show (business email, phone, website, social accounts), plus photos of your work. They appear on the site only after we approve your listing.
  • Private, and that's all: your first name and your answers about licenses and permits. Our review team uses them to help you and decide when a listing is ready. They are never published.
  • Community stories: your first name, business and a public contact so we can confirm with you before publishing.

What we don't ask for

No last name, home address, private phone, date of birth or payment details. We reply to applications through the business contacts you share.

How it is stored

Applications and photos are stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider. The review dashboard is password-protected. Because we keep so little private information, there is very little to protect.

When a business applies or someone sends a Community story, we receive an email alert in our own inbox. These alerts are delivered by Resend, an email delivery service, and contain the business name, category, city and licensing answer, or the story's title and author. Businesses never receive automatic emails from us.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to correct your listing, unpublish it, or delete your application and photos: write to hello@frontstreetmakers.com.

Sponsors

Some local businesses sponsor the site. Sponsored placements are always labeled “Sponsor”. Clicking one takes you to the sponsor's own website, which has its own privacy policy. We don't share your information with sponsors.

Visit statistics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are useful. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you across other websites, and doesn't tell us who you are.

Cookies

We don't use advertising cookies. The site remembers small preferences in your browser, such as daytime or evening on the street.
