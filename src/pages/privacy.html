Privacy notice

Your information, plainly.

We collect only what we need to review and publish free business listings, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Public profile details you choose to share: business name, description, city or service area, links, public email or phone, and photos. These are published only after we approve your listing.
  • Private application details: your name, contact email, phone, ZIP code, and your answers about licensing and permits. These are used only by our review team and are never published.
  • Community stories you send, with your name and email. We contact you before publishing anything, and your email is never shown.

What we never publish

Home addresses, private contact details, licensing answers and internal review notes.

How it is stored

Applications and photos are stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider. Access to the review dashboard is password-protected.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to correct your listing, unpublish it, or delete your application and photos: write to hello@temeculahomegrown.com.

Sponsors

Some local businesses sponsor the site. Sponsored placements are always labeled “Sponsor”. Clicking one takes you to the sponsor's own website, which has its own privacy policy. We don't share your information with sponsors.

Cookies

We don't use advertising cookies. The site remembers small preferences in your browser, such as daytime or evening on the street.
